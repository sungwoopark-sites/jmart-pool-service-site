What We Do

Complete Pool Care Services

From routine weekly maintenance to equipment repairs, we handle every aspect of pool care for homeowners and businesses across Orange County.

Regular Pool Maintenance

Weekly, bi-weekly, and other scheduled maintenance options including chemical balancing, pool inspections, cleaning, and lighting replacement. We bring all equipment and chemicals.

Pool Filter Cleaning

Thorough filter cleaning to remove debris, mold, and obstructions. A clean filter means clean, safe water. We'll remove, clean, and reinstall filters properly — no dirty water mixing in.

Motor Pump Repair

Leaks, strange noises, electrical issues — don't let pump problems go unaddressed. We diagnose and repair pool pumps quickly, using high-quality parts for long-lasting results.

Pool Acid Wash

When your pool has been sitting or is heavily stained, an acid wash restores it to full clarity. We drain and clean completely, getting your pool ready for use.

Pool Lighting Installation

Lighting installation, replacement, and upgrades including LED and spa lights. Intermatic timer upgrades and electrical issue repairs also available.

Equipment & Automation

Equipment installation, salt systems, automation upgrades and repairs, valve replacements, sand filter replacement, pool heater repair — complete equipment care.
